# KruMate OS

## Overview

KruMate OS is an AI-powered Teaching Studio designed for Thai teachers.

The goal is simple:

**Help teachers create teaching materials in minutes instead of hours.**

Teachers can enter a short prompt and instantly generate:

- Lesson Plans
- Worksheets
- Quizzes
- Presentations
- Rubrics
- Classroom Activities

KruMate OS is not an LMS, ERP, or Student Management System.

It is an AI Content Generation Platform focused on reducing lesson preparation time.

---

# Problem

Thai teachers spend significant time preparing:

- Lesson plans
- Worksheets
- Quizzes
- Slides
- Assessments

This reduces the time available for:

- Teaching
- Student engagement
- Classroom activities
- Personalized learning

---

# Solution

Teachers enter a simple prompt.

Example:

> Create a Grade 6 science lesson about ecosystems

AI automatically generates:

- Lesson Plan
- Slides
- Worksheet
- Quiz
- Rubric

within a single workflow.

---

# Product Vision

Transform lesson preparation from:

2–3 hours

into

5–10 minutes.

Target impact:

- Reduce preparation time by 50–70%
- Save 5–7 hours per teacher per week
